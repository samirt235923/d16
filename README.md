# Bangla Comfort Zone

Design and build a high-converting, mobile-first Bangla landing page for a D16 Mini Air Humidifier targeting Bangladeshi customers.



🎯 Main Goal



The primary goal is to convert Facebook/Instagram traffic into Cash on Delivery (COD) orders.



The landing page should feel:



- Trustworthy

- Simple

- Affordable

- Urgent

- Modern

- Suitable for Bangladeshi customers

- Optimized primarily for mobile users



Do NOT make the website look like a complicated international e-commerce store. Keep it simple, familiar and conversion-focused.



---



🇧🇩 Target Audience & Bangladesh Psychology



Target customers are Bangladeshi Facebook users who are interested in:



- Bedroom comfort

- Study table / desk accessories

- Office desk gadgets

- Room decoration

- Small useful gadgets

- Affordable lifestyle products



Use Bangla language throughout the page.



The design should address common Bangladeshi COD customer concerns:



1. “Productটা আসলেই ভালো তো?”

2. “Productটা দেখতে কেমন?”

3. “কিভাবে ব্যবহার করবো?”

4. “Delivery charge কত?”

5. “আমি টাকা আগে দিবো না তো?”

6. “অন্যরা কিনে সন্তুষ্ট হয়েছে কি?”

7. “Offerটা সত্যি কি না?”

8. “Productটা আমার কাজে আসবে তো?”



Therefore, build trust using:



- Real customer feedback screenshots

- Product demonstration video

- How-to-use video

- Clear pricing

- Clear delivery charge

- Cash on Delivery messaging

- Simple order form

- Reassuring microcopy



Avoid fake claims, fake reviews, fake scarcity or exaggerated medical/health claims.



---



🎨 Visual Style



Create a premium but affordable gadget-store aesthetic.



Recommended style:



- Clean white/light background

- Soft blue / cyan accent

- Subtle gradients

- Rounded cards

- Modern Bangla typography

- Large product photography

- Minimal icons

- Strong CTA buttons

- Soft shadows

- Plenty of whitespace



The product should remain the visual hero.



Use a modern Bangla font such as:

Hind Siliguri / Noto Sans Bengali



Make all text highly readable on mobile.



---



📱 Mobile-First Design



Most visitors will come from Facebook on smartphones.



Design for:



- 360px

- 390px

- 412px

- 430px



Mobile should be the primary design, desktop secondary.



Use:



- Large readable Bangla text

- Large CTA buttons

- Short paragraphs

- Swipeable sections where appropriate

- Sticky bottom CTA



Avoid:



- Huge navigation menus

- Long walls of text

- Tiny fonts

- Complex animations

- Slow-loading elements



---

1. HERO SECTION



Above the fold should immediately communicate:



Problem → Product → Offer → CTA



Include:



- Product image

- Product name: “D16 Mini Air Humidifier”

- Short benefit-focused headline

- Small supporting text

- Original price: ৳699

- Discount price: ৳449

- “৳250 সাশ্রয়”

- Countdown timer

- Primary CTA: “অর্ডার করতে চাই”

- Small trust message:

  “ক্যাশ অন ডেলিভারি সুবিধা আছে”

“রুমের বাতাসকে আরও আরামদায়ক করুন ছোট্ট D16 Humidifier দিয়ে 💨”



Do not overpromise medical benefits.



Make the ৳449 price visually dominant.

“🔥 সীমিত সময়ের জন্য বিশেষ ডিসকাউন্ট!”

“সাশ্রয় ৳250”



Add a countdown timer:

ঘণ্টা | মিনিট | সেকেন্ড



The countdown should create urgency but should not look fake or aggressive.



Include CTA below:



“৳449-এ অর্ডার করুন →”

Heading:



“ভিডিওতে দেখে নিন—D16 Humidifier কেমন কাজ করে”



Show the product demonstration video.



Use:



- Rounded video container

- Poster image

- Play button

- Lazy loading



The video should immediately show the product in use.

🛠️ 5. HOW TO USE VIDEO



Create another section:



“মাত্র কয়েকটি ধাপেই ব্যবহার করুন”



Show the how-to-use video.



Under video, show 3 simple steps:

২. USB কানেক্ট করুন

“USB cable সংযুক্ত করুন।”



৩. চালু করুন

“Power button চাপুন এবং mist উপভোগ করুন।”



Use simple numbered cards.



---



❤️ 6. CUSTOMER FEEDBACK



This section is extremely important for Bangladesh COD customers.



Heading:



“যারা ব্যবহার করেছেন, তারা কী বলছেন?”



Display real customer feedback screenshots.



Use:



- Screenshot cards

- Rounded corners

- Slight shadow

- Swipeable horizontal carousel on mobile



Do NOT fabricate reviews.



If screenshots contain customer names/numbers, protect their personal information where necessary.



Add a small trust statement:



“বাস্তব কাস্টমারদের শেয়ার করা feedback”

- ঢাকার বাইরে

“ক্যাশ অন ডেলিভারিতে অর্ডার করুন”

“অর্ডার কনফার্ম করার আগে আমাদের প্রতিনিধি ফোন করে নিশ্চিত করতে পারে।”



Do not ask for unnecessary information.

🧠 CONVERSION UX



Follow these principles:



1. Reduce friction



The customer should understand the product and order process within seconds.



2. Repeat the offer strategically



Show ৳699 → ৳449 at:



- Hero

- Offer section

- Price summary

- Final CTA



3. Build trust before asking for order



Product → Benefits → Video → Customer feedback → Delivery → Order form



4. Make COD obvious



Bangladeshi customers should immediately understand that they can pay upon delivery.



5. Use Bangla microcopy



Avoid unnecessary English e-commerce terminology.



6. Strong visual hierarchy



The eye should naturally move:



Product → ৳449 → Offer → Benefits → Reviews → Order



---



⚡ PERFORMANCE



The landing page will receive Facebook Ads traffic, so performance is critical.



Implement:



- Mobile-first responsive design

- WebP/AVIF product images

- Lazy-load videos

- Lazy-load customer screenshots

- Optimized fonts

- Minimal JavaScript

- No unnecessary libraries

- Fast initial page load

- Good Core Web Vitals

- Avoid autoplay video with sound

- Compress all media



---



🎨 COMPONENT STYLE



Use:



- 12–20px border radius

- Soft shadows

- Large CTA buttons

- Consistent spacing

- Clear section separation

- Premium card UI

- Subtle animations only



CTA buttons should have strong contrast and obvious click affordance.



Use micro-interactions such as:



- Button press animation

- Countdown animation

- Smooth scroll

- Image/video reveal



Do not overuse animations.



---



🖥️ DESKTOP



Desktop layout should use a centered max-width container.



Hero:

Left = product image

Right = offer + countdown + CTA



Benefits:

3–5 cards in grid



Videos:

2-column layout where appropriate



Customer feedback:

Grid/carousel



Order:

Centered, distraction-free order form



---



📱 MOBILE ORDER



On mobile, prioritize:



1. Product image

2. Offer price

3. Countdown

4. CTA

5. Benefits

6. Product video

7. How-to-use video

8. Customer feedback

9. Delivery information

10. Order form

11. FAQ

12. Final CTA



Use a sticky bottom order button.



---



🚫 IMPORTANT



Do NOT:



- Use fake customer reviews

- Use fake purchase notifications

- Use fake scarcity

- Make medical claims

- Claim that humidifier cures diseases

- Use confusing pricing

- Hide delivery charges

- Make the order form complicated

- Overload the page with popups

- Use aggressive flashing animations



The landing page should feel like a trusted Bangladeshi gadget brand, not a low-quality dropshipping page.



Final Design Objective



Create a landing page that makes a Facebook visitor think:



“Productটা useful মনে হচ্ছে → দামটা reasonable → অন্যরা ব্যবহার করেছে → COD আছে → delivery charge clear → order করা সহজ → এখনই order করি.”



The final UI should look modern, trustworthy, premium and highly conversion-focused, while remaining simple enough for Bangladeshi mobile users.
